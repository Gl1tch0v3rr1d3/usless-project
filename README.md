##  About This "Useless" Project

This is a humorous take on CAPTCHA systems where each level introduces new tricks, challenges, and ways to question your very existence. Can you prove you're not AI? Or will you embrace your robotic overlords?

##  Features

- **12 progressively evil levels** - From simple image recognition to existential crises
- **Creative "tricks"** - Moving tiles, changing instructions, impossible timers
- **Sarcastic commentary** - Because why not add emotional damage?
- **Mobile-friendly** - Suffer on any device!
- **Pure frontend** - No servers, no tracking, just pain

## How to Play

1. **Check the box** (because that proves you're human)
2. **Complete each level** by selecting the correct tiles
3. **Don't get tricked** by moving elements, changing instructions, or philosophical questions
4. **Survive all 12 levels** to achieve verified human status
